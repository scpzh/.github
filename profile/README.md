## About SCP-MH and IATT 

SCP Miraheze CN is an unofficial SCP Wiki in Miraheze. Interface Administration and Technical Support Team, aka **IATT**. A subteam of SCP Miraheze CN Staff Team.

SCP基金会Miraheze中文是一个建立在Miraheze的非官方民间维基。界面管理与技术支援小组（IATT）则是本站站务组的一个子小组。


## IATT's Job

- **Maintain the site-wide interface**: Maintain the site-wide CSS, JavaScript, JSON, and other interface code.
- **Maintain associated services**: Maintain status pages, navigation pages, forums, and other services associated with the wiki.
- **Provide technical support**: Provide technical support related to the website to site members.

- **维护全站界面**: 维护全站 CSS、JavaScript、JSON 或其他界面配置代码。
- **维护附属服务**: 维护附属于维基的状态页，导航页和论坛等。
- **提供技术支援**: 为网站成员提供与网站相关的技术支持。
